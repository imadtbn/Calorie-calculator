What's the difference between BMR and TDEE?

These terms appear often when calculating calories, but each has a different meaning.

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator Team

BMR is the basal metabolic rate: the approximate energy the body needs to perform basic functions at rest. TDEE is total daily energy expenditure; it adds the effects of movement, activity, and digestion to the basal rate.

How to interpret the two numbers?

If your BMR shows 1,600 calories, that doesn't mean your daily target is necessarily 1,600 calories. When you add your daily activity, the estimate rises to a value closer to your TDEE.

Think of BMR as the body's running cost, and TDEE as your full-day cost with activity.
