How to interpret a sleep assessment?

Explanation of the sleep-assessment questions and habits that may help improve your routine.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

The assessment measures time to fall asleep, wake times, sleep quality, and its effect on energy and concentration over the past month.

What does the tool measure?

A higher score means there are more indicators that warrant attention, but it does not diagnose insomnia or a sleep disorder. See a doctor if the problem persists or if you have loud snoring or noticeable pauses in breathing.

The electronic result is a starting point for discussion, not a final verdict on your health.

A practical step after the result

Record your symptoms, how long they last, and what affects your day, then share this information with your doctor or specialist. Do not stop medication or start a treatment plan based solely on the questionnaire result.
