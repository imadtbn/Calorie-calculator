Balancing phone use: a short self-check

Questions to help you notice how your phone affects sleep, tasks, and relationships.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

There is no single number that defines healthy use for everyone. What matters is the effect on sleep, focus, relationships, and your ability to stop.

What does the tool measure?

Try notification-free periods, keep your phone away from the bed, and set clear times for browsing. If anxiety or loss of control is severe, discuss it with a professional.

The electronic result is a starting point for discussion, not a final verdict on your health.

A practical step after the result

Record your symptoms, how long they last, and what affects your day, then share this information with your doctor or specialist. Do not stop medication or start a treatment plan based solely on the questionnaire result.
