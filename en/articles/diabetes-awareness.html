Daily diabetes awareness without self-diagnosis

How to make follow-up and healthy habits part of your day without relying on an electronic checklist.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

The checklist helps you remember follow-up, your doctor's plan, activity, food, and hydration, but it does not measure blood sugar or adjust medications.

What does the tool measure?

Repeated unusual readings or symptoms like extreme thirst, frequent urination, and fatigue warrant medical attention. Do not change your treatment without a prescription.

The electronic result is a starting point for discussion, not a final verdict on your health.

A practical step after the result

Record your symptoms, how long they last, and what affects your day, then share this information with your doctor or specialist. Do not stop medication or start a treatment plan based solely on the questionnaire result.
