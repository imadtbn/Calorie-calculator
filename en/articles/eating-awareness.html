Awareness of eating patterns and seeking support

An informational article on signs of eating disorders and how to seek help without stigma.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

Your relationship with food may be affected by stress, body image, and social pressure. Noticing signs is a step, but it isn't enough to diagnose an eating disorder.

What does the tool measure?

Self-induced vomiting, using laxatives to control weight, or repeated loss of control over eating are sufficient reasons to speak with a professional, compassionately and confidentially.

The electronic result is a starting point for discussion, not a final verdict on your health.

A practical step after the result

Record your symptoms, how long they last, and what affects your day, then share this information with your doctor or specialist. Do not stop medication or start a treatment plan based solely on the questionnaire result.
