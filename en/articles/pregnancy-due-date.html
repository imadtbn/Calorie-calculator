Pregnancy and Follow-up

How is the expected delivery date calculated?

The estimated due date helps schedule follow-up, but it is not a guaranteed delivery date.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

The due date calculator uses the first day of your last menstrual period to give an initial estimate of gestational age. In the common method, about 280 days, i.e. 40 weeks, are added to that date. You can try Due date calculator then save the result to discuss with your healthcare provider.

Why isn't the date final?

The rule assumes a relatively regular cycle, but ovulation timing varies between women and cycle length can change month to month. A doctor may therefore adjust the estimate after reviewing your dates and on the basis of examination or ultrasound.

The estimated due date is a reference point for follow-up, not a promise that birth will occur on that day.

What information is useful for the doctor?

Enter the date of the first day of your last period, and note if your cycles are irregular or the date is uncertain. These details help interpret the result better.

When should I seek urgent help?

The calculator is not suitable for assessing pain, bleeding, dizziness, or emergency symptoms. If you have a worrying symptom, seek medical care immediately.
