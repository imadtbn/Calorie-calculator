Simplified guide to macros

Macros are the major nutrients that provide the body with energy and form the basis of meals.

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator Team

When we talk about macros, we usually mean protein, carbohydrates, and fats. Each plays a different role, and there is no one-size-fits-all ratio.

Protein

Protein helps build and repair tissues and promotes satiety. Sources include meat, eggs, legumes, and dairy; individual needs vary.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates provide readily available energy and are found in grains, fruits, vegetables, and legumes. Choosing high-fiber sources can help with satiety.

Fat

Fats play important roles in hormones and the absorption of certain vitamins. They can be obtained from nuts, seeds, oils, and fish.

Macro distribution is a tool for organization, not a test of success or failure. Food quality, how you feel, and consistency also matter.
