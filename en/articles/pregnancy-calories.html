Calories during pregnancy: how to interpret the estimate?

Calorie estimates during pregnancy are an educational starting point, not an individualized dietary prescription.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

The phrase "eating for two" does not mean doubling calories. Pregnancy calorie calculator Adds a general amount to baseline calories by trimester to illustrate the concept, not to create an individualized diet plan.

What's the approximate increase?

Reliable health sources note the first trimester often does not require a fixed increase, while a general increase may be about 340 calories/day in the second trimester and about 450 calories/day in the third for singleton pregnancies. Needs vary by pre-pregnancy weight, activity, weight gain, appetite, and health status.

The approximate number doesn't reflect food quality and does not replace prenatal care.

What makes a meal nutritious?

Choose a variety of nutrient-dense foods, and discuss supplements, fluids, caffeine, and food safety with your healthcare provider. Don't start a supplement or a therapeutic diet without guidance.

When should I not rely on the calculator alone?

Do not rely on it for multiple pregnancy, gestational diabetes, severe vomiting, weight loss, or when there is a specific medical recommendation. In those cases, follow the care team's plan.
