Mood questionnaire: when should I seek help?

Explanation of mood screening over the past two weeks with a safety alert for self-harm thoughts.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

Questions cover interest, pleasure, mood, sleep, energy, and concentration. The result is a preliminary screen, not a diagnosis or a substitute for clinical evaluation.

What does the tool measure?

If you answered that you have thoughts of death or self-harm, do not stay alone: contact local emergency services or a trusted person immediately and seek urgent evaluation.

The electronic result is a starting point for discussion, not a final verdict on your health.

A practical step after the result

Record your symptoms, how long they last, and what affects your day, then share this information with your doctor or specialist. Do not stop medication or start a treatment plan based solely on the questionnaire result.
