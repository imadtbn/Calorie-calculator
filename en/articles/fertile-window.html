Fertility and ovulation window: how to interpret the estimate?

A calm review of your cycle helps you spot patterns, with a reminder that ovulation doesn't occur on the same day for everyone.

Last updated: 22 August 2026 · Prepared by: Calorie Calculator team

Estimates Fertility window calculator Possible days based on the first day of your last period and your average cycle length. The calculator makes a rough estimate assuming ovulation occurs about 14 days before the next period, then shows an approximate range around it.

Why use a range instead of a single day?

Sperm can live several days in the reproductive tract, and ovulation can come earlier or later. For that reason the tool presents an approximate window rather than a single moment, and it does not guarantee pregnancy.

When is the calculation less accurate?

Accuracy is reduced with irregular cycles, after hormonal changes, or with stress, illness, disrupted sleep, or weight changes. Record dates for several months and don't rely on guesswork alone.

The calendar shows a probability; it does not confirm ovulation and is not reliable on its own as contraception.

When should I consult a specialist?

If cycles are very irregular or absent, or if pregnancy is delayed, a gynecologist can assess the situation and suggest more appropriate options.
