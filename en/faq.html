Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how the tools work and the limits of their results.

Are the calorie calculator results accurate?

They are estimates based on general formulas. Use them as a starting point, monitor your response over time, and consult a professional if needed.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Basic tools run directly in the browser and don't require sign-up or entering personal data.

What's the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the body's approximate resting requirement, while TDEE adds the effect of activity and daily movement. Read the explanatory article.

Can I use the result to lose weight?

It can serve as a general reference, but don't follow a severe deficit or a treatment plan without professional supervision, especially if you have a medical condition or a history of eating disorders.

Are the values in the food table fixed?

No. Values vary by item, size, and cooking method, so we present them as approximate values per 100 g.

Do you use Arabic or Latin numerals?

Results and values are shown using Latin digits 0-9 to make reading and comparison easier.
