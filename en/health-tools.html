How to use health tools safely?

Read instructions and enter accurate information, then treat the result as a starting point for discussion or education. Do not change medications or therapeutic diets or delay seeking care because of an electronic tool's result.

Important warning: If you have severe or emergency symptoms, call emergency services or visit the nearest health facility instead of using calculators.
Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about health tools and calculators

Brief answers to help you understand the tool's limitations and use it more wisely.

What types of tools are included in the guide?

The guide includes calculators, questionnaires, and health checklists that run on-site; they aim to educate and help understand indicators, not to provide diagnoses.

How do I choose the right tool?

Choose the tool closest to your question, read its instructions and limits, then use the result as a starting point for discussion or habit change.

Are the results of medical tools final?

No. Results are estimative or for awareness, and may require lab measurements, clinical examination, or specialist follow-up depending on the topic.

When should I not use the tool?

Do not delay emergency care or change a medication or therapeutic diet because of an electronic tool's result. If symptoms are severe, seek care immediately.

Warning: This information is general educational material and does not replace a doctor's assessment or emergency services when needed.
