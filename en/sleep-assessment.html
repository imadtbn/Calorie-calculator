Educational screening

Sleep awareness assessment

Think about your usual sleep pattern during the past month, then choose the closest answer for each question.

1. How long does it usually take you to fall asleep?
2. If you wake up at night, how long are you awake in total?
3. How many nights per week do you have trouble sleeping?
4. How do you rate your sleep quality?
5. To what extent has poor sleep affected your mood, energy, or relationships?
6. To what extent has poor sleep affected your concentration, productivity, or alertness?
7. How much of a problem is your sleep overall?
8. How long have you been experiencing sleep problems?
ⓘThis result does not diagnose a medical condition or replace a healthcare professional.

How to read the result?

The result is an awareness indicator based on your answers, not a diagnosis or final judgment. If symptoms persist or affect your life, book an appointment with your doctor or an appropriate specialist.

Warning: If you have severe or emergency symptoms, seek urgent care instead of relying on any online questionnaire.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about the Sleep Awareness Assessment

Brief answers to help you understand the tool's limitations and use it more wisely.

What time period does the sleep assessment cover?

It covers eight questions about sleep quality and its effects during the past month, aiming to raise awareness of reported habits and symptoms.

Does the assessment result diagnose a sleep disorder?

No. The assessment is an awareness tool and does not replace taking a medical history or evaluating causes of insomnia, snoring, and fatigue by a specialist.

What is the first step after a concerning result?

Review your sleep schedule, stimulants, light exposure, and screen use; record your symptoms; then discuss ongoing problems or daytime effects with your doctor.

When should I seek a professional evaluation?

Seek evaluation if symptoms persist, if you experience excessive sleepiness, snoring with breathing pauses, or if sleep affects driving or work.

Warning: This information is general educational material and does not replace a doctor's assessment or emergency services when needed.
