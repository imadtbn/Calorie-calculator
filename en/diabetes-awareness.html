Frequently asked questions

FAQ: Daily Diabetes Awareness Checklist

Brief answers to help you understand the tool's limitations and use it more wisely.

Does the daily awareness checklist diagnose diabetes?

No. It's a checklist for daily habits and monitoring; it does not measure blood sugar or diagnose diabetes or prediabetes.

What can be monitored daily?

You can record meals, activity, prescribed medications, and blood sugar readings if you have a testing plan, plus notes to discuss with your care team.

Should I change my medication based on the checklist?

No. Do not change doses or stop medication based on this checklist; follow your doctor's plan and seek clarification if unusual readings recur.

When should I seek urgent care?

Seek urgent care for confusion, fainting, persistent vomiting, abnormal breathing, or rapid deterioration — do not wait to complete the checklist.

Warning: This information is general educational material and does not replace a doctor's assessment or emergency services when needed.
