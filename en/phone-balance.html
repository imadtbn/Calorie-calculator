Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about the Phone Balance Questionnaire

Brief answers to help you understand the tool's limitations and use it more wisely.

What does the Phone Balance review measure?

The short questions help notice the impact of digital habits on sleep, focus, tasks, and stress, without diagnosing addiction or disorder.

Is there a suitable usage duration for everyone?

There isn't one number that fits everyone; what's more important is the effect of use on sleep, work, relationships, and the ability to stop.

What's a simple step to improve balance?

Start by setting a phone-free period before bedtime, turning off unnecessary notifications, and putting your phone away during one task each day.

When should I seek professional support?

Seek support if use causes persistent problems, loss of control, severe anxiety, or impacts safety, sleep, or relationships.

Warning: This information is general educational material and does not replace a doctor's assessment or emergency services when needed.
