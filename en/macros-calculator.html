Frequently asked questions

Macro Calculator FAQ

Brief answers to help you understand the tool's limitations and use it more wisely.

What are macros?

Macros are the major nutrients: protein, carbohydrates, and fats. This calculator shows an approximate way to split daily energy among them.

Is there an ideal macro ratio for everyone?

There is no one-size-fits-all ratio; needs depend on age, activity, goals, health status, and food preferences.

Should you follow the grams exactly?

No. Use the numbers as a flexible guide for building balanced meals, and focus on food quality, satiety, and sustainability rather than chasing an exact daily number.

Can the calculator be used for therapeutic purposes?

It is not recommended without specialist supervision, especially with diabetes, kidney disease, pregnancy, eating disorders, or any prescribed therapeutic diet.
