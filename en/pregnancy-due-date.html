Understanding the result

What does the estimated due date tell you?

The estimated due date is a reference for scheduling care, not a guaranteed delivery date. Normal pregnancy length varies between women, and early ultrasound can help improve the gestational age estimate.

1

Start date

The calculator uses the first day of the last menstrual period, as commonly used to calculate gestational age.

40

Approximate weeks

An estimate of 280 days or 40 weeks is used, which may vary if cycles are irregular.

✓

Follow-up is important

Your clinician confirms gestational age and the follow-up plan based on history, exam, and imaging if needed.

When is the calculation not sufficient on its own?

The calculation may be less accurate if cycles are irregular, the last period date is unknown, or conception occurred after stopping hormonal contraception. In these cases, tell your healthcare provider the details.

Warning: If you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, dizziness, or any worrying symptom, seek medical care instead of relying on an online calculator.
Frequently asked questions

Questions about the due date

Is the calculated due date certain?

No. It's an initial estimate based on the first day of the last period, and it may change depending on cycle length, exam findings, ultrasound results, and your clinician's assessment.

How does the calculator compute the expected date?

The calculator adds about 280 days to the first day of the last period, i.e., 40 weeks.

When should I see a doctor?

See your healthcare provider to confirm gestational age and the follow-up plan, especially if you have pain, bleeding, or concerning symptoms.
