Privacy & Ads Policy

This page explains what happens when you use the site's tools and how advertising services and external links may work.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

We try to keep calculations simple and local whenever possible. This policy explains the practices of the Calorie Calculator site, but it does not replace legal requirements in your country or consent settings you may need to enable in your Google AdSense account.

What data do you enter into the calculators?

You enter values like age, weight, height, activity level, or weight and height for BMI and water. These values are used inside your browser to show an estimated result; the site does not require creating an account or sending these values to our server.

Local storage and cookies

The current version does not save a persistent record of results via a user account. Your browser may use basic technologies to run the site, and third-party services may use cookies or similar techniques under their independent settings.

Google AdSense and ads

The site uses Google AdSense to show ads on some pages. Google and its partners may use cookies, web signals, IP addresses, or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads and prevent invalid activity; personalized ads may vary by user settings and local regulations.

For more information, read How Google uses your information in adsand you can manage personalized ads from Ad Settings CenterPrivacy consent messages from Google may also appear when enabled in the publisher's account for the region.

ads.txt file

The site contains an ads.txt to identify advertisers and parties authorized to sell ad inventory. This file does not store personal information about visitors.

External Links

Articles may include links to health sources or external services. When you open those links, the external party's privacy policy and terms apply, and the site does not control those parties' cookies or practices.

Children and sensitive data

The site is not intended to collect data from children or sensitive health data. Do not post medical information, ID numbers, or data you don't need for handling a technical report on contact pages.

Your rights and privacy requests

You can delete local browsing data from your browser settings, and manage cookies and personalized ads from Google and your browser settings. To request clarification or report an issue, use Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page if we add analytics, forms, or new advertising services. We'll show the last updated date at the top of the page and explain material changes when possible.

The calculators are estimates for education and should not be used as medical advice or for submitting private health data.
