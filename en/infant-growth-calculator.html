What does the calculator measure?

The calculator compares recumbent length and optional weight by infant age and sex to WHO tables from birth to 24 months, and shows the Z-score and approximate percentile for each index.

How is the result measured?

The calculator uses LMS parameters from WHO tables to compute a Z-score, then converts it to an approximate percentile. The nearest month from official length/weight tables is selected; results may differ slightly from tools using age in days or interpolating between months.

When to seek help? If you notice continued slowing of growth, feeding difficulties, weight loss, or frequent vomiting, contact your pediatrician instead of relying on the calculator.
Frequently asked questions

Questions about infant growth

What age range does the calculator cover?

This version covers infants from birth to 24 months and uses recumbent length per the WHO length-for-age table.

Does the result diagnose growth delay?

No. The result is an initial reference comparison, not a diagnosis. Growth assessment requires accurate, repeated measurements and pediatric review.

Is measuring length alone sufficient?

No. A doctor may need to review weight, head circumference, nutrition, medical history, and growth velocity, so do not rely on a single reading.
