Our story

We simplify numbers without reducing the person to them.

The Calorie Calculator is an Arabic educational platform that helps you understand certain nutritional indicators using simple, fast tools.

What do we offer?

We provide estimate calculators for calories, BMI, and macros, along with a simplified food table and short articles. The goal is to give you a shared language to start a better conversation with yourself or a specialist.

How we work

Calculations are performed in your browser using JavaScript; the tools do not need to send your data to a server. We designed the components to be extensible so future tools can be added without rebuilding the site from scratch.

What we don't provide

We do not provide diagnoses, medical prescriptions, or a substitute for a physician or dietitian. General figures do not always capture differences in age, illness, muscle mass, and daily activity.

Use the site as a tool for understanding, and make important health decisions with a qualified professional who knows your full context.
