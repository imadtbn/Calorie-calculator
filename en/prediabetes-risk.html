Early awareness

What factors are included in the estimate?

The test assigns approximate points for age, sex, family history, blood pressure, physical activity, and prior gestational diabetes, plus a BMI category. Your answers are not sent to a server.

The score is not a diagnostic test.

Blood sugar may be normal or high regardless of the questionnaire result; laboratory testing remains the appropriate reference.

Activity and nutrition

Regular activity and balanced food choices may support overall health, but an individual plan should be discussed with a professional.

When should I get tested?

If the result is high, or if you have risk factors or symptoms, contact your doctor to determine the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the prediabetes risk test

Does the result diagnose diabetes?

No. This is an estimate of risk factors, not a blood test or a diagnosis. If the result is high, talk with your doctor about appropriate tests.

What does a total of 5 points or more mean?

According to the CDC method used here, this indicates a higher risk of having prediabetes and warrants discussing testing with your doctor.

Is a low score enough to be reassured?

No. A low score does not eliminate risk factors and does not replace testing if symptoms are present or if testing is medically recommended.
